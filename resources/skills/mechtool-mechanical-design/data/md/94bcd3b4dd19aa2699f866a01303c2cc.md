#### 零件结构要适应刀具尺寸
注 意 事 项
图 例
说 明
改 进 前
改 进后
应考虑刀具退出时所需的退刀槽
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability67.jpg)
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability68.jpg)
1．保证刀具能自由退刀
2．避免刀具损坏和过早磨损
3．提高加工质量
4．避免设备事故
当尺寸差别不大时，零件各结构要素，如沟、槽、孔、窝等，应尽可能一致
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability69.jpg)
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability70.jpg)
1．减少刀具种类
2．减少更换刀具等辅助时间
应考虑刀具能正常地进刀和退刀
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability71.jpg)
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability72.jpg)
尽可能避免在斜面上钻孔和钻不完整孔，以防止刀具损坏和提高加工精度及切削用量
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability73.jpg)
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability74.jpg)
应保证砂轮自由退出和加工的空间
尽可能采用标准刀具
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability75.jpg)
![图](https://www.mechdoc.cn/images/partstructure/partstructure_cuttingpartstructuredesignprocessability76.jpg)
尽量不采用接长钻头等非标准刀具
