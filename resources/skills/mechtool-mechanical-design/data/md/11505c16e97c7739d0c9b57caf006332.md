计算公式:
E=E1*10^6
α=a/ι,ωτα=α^2-α^3
RA=P/2*(2-3*α+3*α^2)

QD=-RB
fC=P*a^2*ι*(3-5*α+3*ωτα)/(12*E*I)
θA=P*a*ι*(1-α)/(4*E*I)

Qx1=RA
Mx1=RA*x
fx1=(RA*(3*ι^2*x-x^3)-3*P*(ι^2-2*a*ι+2*a^2)*x)/(6*E*I)
CD段(a≤x≤ι-a):
Qx2=RA-P
Mx2=RA*x-P*(x-a)
fx2=(RA*(3*ι^2*x-x^3)-3*P*(ι^2-2*a*ι+2*a^2)*x+P*(x-a)^3)/(6*E*I)
DB段(x≥ι-a):
Qx3=RA-2*P
Mx3=RA*x-P*(2*x-ι)
fx3=(RA*(3*ι^2*x-x^3-2*ι^3)+P*(ι^3-3*ι*x^2+2*x^3))/(6*E*I)

Qx3=RA-q*c
Mx3=RA*x-q*c*(x-a)
fx3=(4*RA*(3*ι^2*x-x^3)-12*q*c*b^2*x+4*q*c*(x-a)^3-q*a*c^3)/(24*E*I)
