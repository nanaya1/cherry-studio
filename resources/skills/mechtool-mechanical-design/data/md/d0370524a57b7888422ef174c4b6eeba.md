MA=-p*a*b^2/ι^2
MB=-p*a^2*b/ι^2
MC=2*p*a^2*b^2/ι^3
QC=-RA(RA>RB时)或-RB
fC=p*a^3*b^3/(3*E*I*ι^3)
a>b时:x1=2*a*ι/(3*a+b),fmax=2*p/(3*E*I)*a^3*b^3/(3*a+b)^2
AC段(x≤a):
Qx1=RA
Mx1=MA+RA*x
fx1=p*b^2*x^2/(6*E*I*ι)*(3*α-(1+2*α)*ξ)
CB段(x≥a):
Qx2=RA-p
Mx2=MA+RA*x-p*(x-a)
fx2=p*a^2*(ι-x)^2/(6*E*I*ι)*((1+2*β)*ξ-α)
设置项:
结果小数位数可选择0-15位(默认12位)
